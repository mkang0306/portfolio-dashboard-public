# Portfolio Thesis

## Horizon and Structure

Write your investment thesis here. This is the canonical document that the dashboard reads.
The Discover tab's thesis-fit scan uses this to score new candidates.

Example structure:

- **Time horizon**: 20+ years
- **Asset allocation**: 100% equity (adjust for your risk tolerance)
- **Core bets**: describe your 2-3 layered bets (broad index, sector conviction, satellites)

## Account Placement

- **Roth**: high-turnover funds (factor ETFs, active managers) -- tax drag in taxable, free in Roth
- **Taxable**: index ETFs (tax-efficient by construction), individual stocks (sale-timing control + loss harvesting), international funds (foreign tax credit)

## Discipline Rules

1. Don't sell on volatility. Only on thesis breaks, with the trigger written down before the position changes.
2. Rebalance via new contributions before selling -- minimize taxable events.
3. Quarterly review with your chosen drift threshold.
4. Position caps: define your maximum single-position weight.
